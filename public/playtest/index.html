Cliffside

Walk a painted cliffside and throw spells. Choose who you play as first.

Current build: three characters, one scene — Warlord (pixel art, the only one who can attack), Keeper (painted) and Necromancer (painted, and missing his three north-facing directions). Pick one on the select screen; each downloads only its own character. Tab / VFX button still cycles the six effects, all of them our own authored work.

Choose a character

About a 37–42 MB first download depending on who you pick, so Wi-Fi is best. Hold your phone sideways (landscape).

On a phone

  • Left thumb anywhere on the left half: move. Push far to run.
  • WHIRLWIND (Warlord only) is a hold — keep your thumb down to keep spinning, and push the stick with your other thumb to spin while walking.
  • CAST throws the current effect, JUMP jumps, VFX switches to the next of the six effects.

On a computer

  • WASD or arrows move, Shift runs, Space jumps.
  • E or left click casts, Tab switches to the next effect.
  • F attacks — Warlord only; the other two ignore it.

Cliffside — IlluminatedA / B

The same cliffside painted two ways, in one build. One button swaps between them so they can be compared on the same spot.

A is the cliffside above: the Keeper, props, particles and glows. B is the Illuminated repaint — a knight with a pollaxe walking and running in all eight directions, an angel and a demon on the slope, and a new burning sky. It opens on B.

Open the A / B build

About a 105 MB first download — it carries both paintings — so Wi-Fi, please. Hold your phone sideways (landscape).

On a phone

  • Left thumb anywhere on the left half: move. Push far to run, or hold RUN.
  • T (marked STYLE) swaps the two cliffsides. G (marked RIG) swaps the east-facing knight between the jointed rig and the painted cells.
  • CAST throws the current effect, JUMP jumps, VFX switches effect.

On a computer

  • WASD or arrows move, Shift runs, Space jumps.
  • T swaps style, G swaps the east-facing rig, Tab switches effect, E or left click casts.

The strip along the bottom of the screen always says which style and which rig you are looking at.

The Frost King's Barrow3D

A barbarian in a winter barrow: standing stones, a frozen tarn, and heather in the wind. Wade through the snow and use the axe and shield.

The phone build of the desktop scene. It uses a lighter renderer, so the ink line is simpler and the heather is painted rather than built from stems.

Open the Barrow

About a 43 MB first download, so Wi-Fi is best. Hold your phone sideways (landscape).

On a phone

  • Left thumb anywhere on the left half: move. Push far to run.
  • SLASH, CHOP and BASH strike. Hold BLOCK to keep the shield up.
  • GEAR (top right) steps through his kit. He starts fully armed, because CHOP, BASH and BLOCK need the axe and shield.

On a computer

  • WASD or arrows move, Shift runs.
  • Space or left click slashes, X chops, C bashes, and B or right click blocks.
  • G steps through his kit.
