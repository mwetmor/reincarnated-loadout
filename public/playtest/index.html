Cliffside

Walk the Keeper across a painted cliffside and throw a Frozen Orb.

Current effect: Frozen Orb v3 — hybrid-spec test (halo glow, floor light, impact flash). Our own authored effect; there is one effect set in this build.

Play Cliffside

About a 45 MB first download, so Wi-Fi is best. Hold your phone sideways (landscape).

On a phone

  • Left thumb anywhere on the left half: move. Push far to run.
  • CAST throws the Frozen Orb, JUMP jumps, VFX switches effect set (only one in this build).

On a computer

  • WASD or arrows move, Shift runs, Space jumps.
  • E or left click casts, Tab switches effect set.
